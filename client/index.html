        How to play

        Your squad (1 to 8 friends) fights through the jungle, across the river and into the enemy fortress. Reach the end and destroy the Iron Fortress together to win.

        Controls

        Run AD or ←→
        Aim up / down WS or ↑↓ (hold with left/right to aim diagonally)
        Jump Space (also K/Z) · hold for higher
        Fire J (also X) · hold to keep shooting
        Double jump Jump again in the air
        Dash Shift (also L/C) · bullets pass through you; one in the air
        Slide ↓ + Dash · slip under shots and knock troopers over
        Knife F (also V/I) · hits 5× harder, breaks shields, swats bullets away. Firing up close knifes automatically.
        Super move Q (also E/O) when the yellow bar is full
        Astra / power R (also G/U) · missions 5–6: fire the astra you picked up; with none, call down the squad BRAHMASTRA when its bar is full
        Auto-fire T or the AUTO FIRE button · advanced missions start with it off
        Drop down ↓ + Jump on a wooden platform
        Phone Left thumb: run and aim (pull down + Dash to slide). Right thumb: Jump, Dash, Knife, SUPER, ASTRA. Tap AUTO FIRE (top right) to switch auto-fire; advanced missions start with it off. Settings can swap the sides for left-handers.

        Fight with style

        • Health bar: 100 HP. A bullet takes about 10. Stay out of fire for a few seconds and it refills.
        • Combo: keep hitting without getting hit. Ranks go C → B → A → S → SS → SSS, and a big combo multiplies your score (up to ×3). Knife kills, slide kills, air kills and parries look best.
        • Super moves: every hero has one. Blaze & Kiki: Overdrive. Nova & Sparky: a shockwave that wipes enemy fire. Tusk & Sarge: an airstrike. Viper & Ghost: a phantom strike that slashes the nearest enemies.
        • SYNC: two friends firing super moves together both hit 50% harder.
        • KO: you are back beside your squad 3 seconds later, using one of the squad's shared lives. Beating a boss gives a life back.

        Bosses

        • WARHOUND (walking tank): double jump or dash through its charge, jump its shockwaves.
        • VULTURE (gunship): keep moving under the bombs, shoot or knife its missiles.
        • IRON FORTRESS: jump the low laser, duck or slide under the high one. Three phases.

        Every mission has its own world and its own bosses, but they fight the same way: the beast charges and stomps like WARHOUND, the flyer bombs like VULTURE, the final boss fires low and high beams like the IRON FORTRESS. Mission 2: Giant Scorpion, Sun Falcon, Anubis Colossus. Mission 3: Fenrir, Thor, Frost Giant. Mission 4: Minotaur, Storm Harpy, Zeus. Mission 5: Inferno Beast, Fire Dragon, Demon King. Mission 6: Xeno Crawler, UFO Mothership, Hive Queen.

        Weapons

        • S Spread: five bullets in a fan
        • M Machine gun: very fast fire
        • L Laser: goes through every enemy in a line
        • A KO costs your special weapon, so dodge!

        Joining late

        Open an invite link any time, even mid-mission: you appear next to your friends and the squad gets one more life.

        Privacy

        STRIKE SQUAD collects as little as possible:

        • Your callsign is shown to players in your squad and kept in the server's memory while you are connected, plus a short reconnect window.
        • A random session code is kept in this browser tab so you can rejoin after a dropped connection. It never appears in links.
        • Your settings, callsign and progress are saved in this browser. No analytics, ads, accounts or emails.
        • On CrazyGames, videos and accounts are run by CrazyGames under its own privacy policy. If you are signed in there, your progress is saved to your CrazyGames account; this game itself collects no analytics or emails.
        • Squads and scores live in server memory only and disappear when a squad ends. Your IP address is used briefly for anti-spam limits and is not stored in a database.

## What each suite proves

- **`shared/tests/sim.test.ts`**: the level (one screen tall; an ambush arena and three boss arenas, each gate owned by one arena; every checkpoint, pick-up, arena and boss reachable by a single-jump graph); physics and moves (landing, 3-tile jump yes / 4-tile no, **double jump** reaches 4 tiles once per jump, one-way platforms and drop-through, short hop, walls and gates, **dash** distance/cooldown, **air dash** once and flat, **slide** low and jump-cancel, input cleaning); countdown; lives formula; one super move per hero type; determinism with all moves; shooting and kills; weapons; **100 HP** (≥10 soldier hits to KO, flinch window); regeneration; **dash i-frames**; **knife** damage and auto-knife; **parry**; **shield troopers** (front blocked, back hits, knife breaks it); **slide** knock-down; grenade blasts; lag compensation and its clamp; **super moves** (meter, nova, overdrive, airstrike, phantom, boss reduction, SYNC); **combo** (ranks, expiry, broken by a hit, score multiplier); KO and respawn with a team life; failure with no lives; waiting for a boss kill to come back; pits; med-kit; checkpoints; drop-in; **arenas** (lock, waves from both sides, gate opens, heal, boss sleeps until locked, health scaling, boss kill opens gate + life); **bosses** (WARHOUND charge tell then rush, dash through it unhurt; stomp shockwaves, jumped; VULTURE bombs and missiles shot down; IRON FORTRESS low/high beams vs jump/duck, phase 2 drones, win); enemies sleeping, ledges, jetpack bursts; snapshot size with 8 players; and **a squad of two computer players beating all three bosses**.
- **`server/tests/integration/multiplayer.test.ts`**: real WebSocket clients against a real `GameServer` with a manual tick clock. Squad create/join/leave/full (8, in lobby and mid-mission), seats and colours, host-only difficulty, solo start, movement replication to all clients, server-side kills, KO and respawn with a team life, ambush arena opening its gate for everyone, dash/knife/super over the network (a tap inside one tick still counts), drop-in mid-mission (+1 life, spawns beside the squad, can move at once), failure and victory with stats reaching everyone, play-again and back-to-lobby, late joiner between missions, empty mission closed, resume with token, disconnected player stops, grace expiry, expired squads, no controlling others, isolated squads, "Play now" (starts at once, next player drops in, never into abandoned public missions, never into private squads), and the real-time timer loop.
- **`server/tests/integration/security.test.ts`**: malformed/binary/oversized frames, schema violations (including non-integer axes and unknown fields), prototype pollution, input flooding (movement still bounded by physics), lag-comp `vt` validation and clamp, stale/duplicate inputs, fire cooldown under spam, jump spam cannot fly (one double jump only), forged damage/kills/score/lives/meter/combo/result, super move only with a full server-side meter, unauthorized options/starts, code guessing, creation limits, per-IP caps, sessions and tokens, origin allow-list, production config refusal, health endpoint.
- **`client/tests/client.test.ts`**: interpolation (no sliding on teleports, bullet extrapolation), 8-way stick mapping, prediction equal to the shared physics, reconciliation/replay, smoothing vs snapping, no prediction while down, settings sanitising, reconnect backoff, every element id used by the code exists in `index.html`, no catch-all `_redirects`.
- **`e2e/game.spec.ts`** (real Chromium): menu and clean console; invalid/unknown codes; solo training (run, kill an enemy, pause really freezes the sim, quit); solo moves (Shift dash, F knife, Q super with a full meter); solo with 3 buddies (labelled bots that move); **two browsers**: host creates, friend joins by invite link, both see each other, the friend's movement replicates, the mission ends for both with a squad report, play again; reconnect to the same seat; "Play now" twice → same mission; drop-in mid-mission appears beside the host; lag-comp `vt` in real inputs; wake-up screen; installable app; no translate popups; phone: controls shown (Jump, Fire, Dash, Knife, SUPER), **every move with real multi-touch** (dash, stick-down + Dash slide, two-tap double jump, knife, SUPER; passed 10 of 10 repeats), the five buttons fit and never overlap each other or the stick on 667×375, 740×360 and 915×412 screens, real multi-touch stick + Jump + auto-fire in solo and online (server-confirmed movement and jump), portrait prompt.

## Bugs found by tests and runs (and fixed)

1. **Unreachable platforms**: three platforms (a gate switch and two boss-arena ledges) were 4 tiles up, higher than a jump. Found while reviewing the printed level; the reachability test now guards it.
2. **Squad power applied one tick late**: shots were fired before squad power was computed. Found by a unit test.
3. **Jump-spam test tripped the input rate limit** (40 inputs in a burst of 30): the test was wrong, not the server; shortened to 25 ticks, still a full jump arc.
4. **Computer players flip-flopped at a fixed distance from a gate** and stood still forever (4- and 8-player squads 0% wins). Found by `npm run playtest`; gate area now starts at the first switch.
5. **Computer players changed their climbing plan mid-air** and never reached the high switch (solo stuck at gate 2). Found by playtest; the plan is now fixed on take-off.
6. **Computer buddies ran ahead and left a downed human behind.** Seen in screenshots; they now turn back for friends down within 30 tiles and wait for the rearmost friend.
7. **Off-screen friend arrows overlapped** when two friends were at the same height. Seen in screenshots; markers now stack.
8. **Drop-in spawned at the level start when the only friend was down** (e2e failed 3 of 6 repeats). Now you appear beside the downed friend so you can revive them; new unit test, and the e2e passed 8 of 8 repeats afterwards.
9. **Phone e2e missed short jumps** at 4× server speed because positions were sampled every 40 ms from the test runner. Sampling moved into the page (10 ms).
10. **Menu logo showed a white box** (the focus ring of the heading that receives focus for screen readers). Removed the ring for programmatic focus only.
11. **Lobby text showed through the sticky Start bar.** Made the bar opaque.
12. **Phones showed tiny characters** (the full 576 px level height on a 412 px screen). The camera now zooms to 400 px and follows vertically on short screens.

13. **Computer players flip-flopped facing every tick** next to an arena edge (moving 7 px each way), so every shot went the wrong way and solo runs stalled forever (3 of 10 easy runs). Found by playtest; the movement dead band is now wider than one step.
14. **WARHOUND landed on a one-way platform** during its stomp, so its shockwaves rolled through the air. Found by a unit test; bosses now ignore one-way platforms.
15. **Missiles could not be shot down** at full speed (the shot jumped past the missile between checks). Found by a unit test; shoot-downs are now tested inside the bullet sub-steps.
16. **A dash could not get through a charging WARHOUND** (the i-frames ended a tick too early). Found by a unit test; dash i-frames last 0.12 s longer than the dash.
17. **Bosses under fire were drawn solid white** (hit flash every frame). Seen in screenshots; the flash is now a light tint.
18. **On phones the gunship was cut off** above the zoomed view. Seen in screenshots; the camera tilts up for it without losing your soldier.
19. **Phone combo meter overlapped the notices.** Seen in screenshots; it moved under the health card.

20. **Buttons hid the boss on small phones** (667×375). Seen in screenshots; buttons are now see-through until pressed, and a tighter layout is used on screens under 380 px tall.

21. **Computer players stood under a turret on top of a wall forever**, aiming straight up into the rock (stage 5, every run). Found by playtest during the stage redesign; bots now only stop for enemies they can see.
22. **Computer players stopped moving in mid-air to aim** at a jetpack and dropped short into low-gravity pits. Found by playtest; bots keep moving until they land.
23. **Computer players double-jumped over the next pillar** (they took any lower ground ahead for a pit) and fell into the gap behind it (stage 6: 137 falls in 6 solo runs). Found by playtest; a double jump is only for a real pit.
24. **An arena wave trooper that wandered out past the arena edge was ignored** by the bots while it shelled them (stage 4: 36 deaths in 6 runs). Found by playtest; the arena's own troopers count wherever they stand.
25. **Computer players flip-flopped facing at the edge of their spot** in the new arena shapes and stalled solo runs. Found by playtest; once on its spot a bot now holds it until the spot moves 2 tiles.
26. **A cave shelf sat under a stalactite** with less headroom than a soldier, so bots (and players) got stuck on it. Found by playtest; the shelf is lower.
27. **A stash pocket in a cave roof could trap a soldier** (rock on both sides, the only way out was drop-through). Found by playtest; its floor now stops short so you walk off it.

28. **Upright phones could not reach Stages, Controls or Settings**: the menu was taller than the screen and could not scroll (iPhone 13: buttons 10 px below the bottom; 360×640: 85 px). Reported by the owner, reproduced in Playwright; the menu panel now scrolls, and the e2e test covers 390×664 and 360×640.
29. **In a mission on an upright phone the touch buttons covered the world and the HUD overlapped itself.** Seen in screenshots; upright phones now get a console layout (the view on top, a control deck under it) and a compact HUD.
30. **A final boss was drawn with the old art** because the look was looked up by arena bounds and a final boss stands past its arena's edge. Seen in screenshots; the nearest boss arena is used.
31. **A portal effect drew an ellipse with a negative radius** in its first frame (a console error). Seen in the browser log; radii are clamped.
32. **Boss fights stuttered: a shaded boss cost about 35 ms a frame** on a canvas drawn without a graphics card. Found by profiling an eight-player boss fight (median frame 57 ms). A boss's picture is now kept between frames and the renderer's quality governor redraws it every second or third frame on a slow machine (`Renderer.govern`, `pixelated(..., keep)`).
33. **Two full-screen gradient fills and a vignette every frame cost about 20 ms** (sky, sun glow, colour grade), more than everything else in the scene together. Found by switching drawing steps off one at a time. They are painted once per zone into pictures and copied (`Renderer.sky`, `Renderer.grade`). Open-field frame: 42 ms → 21 ms; eight-player boss fight: 57 ms → 20 ms (headless Chromium, 1920x1080, software rendering).
34. **The rim light on boss parts used a clip per part** (about 70 save/clip/restore a frame). Replaced by stroking only the edges that face the light (`asura-shade.ts`).
35. **Two burning troopers standing together lit each other for ever.** Found by the new agni test (a trooper was still alight long after the fire should have died). The fire is now handed on twice at most, and never to one that has only just stopped burning.
36. **A "thunderbolt" wave left troopers standing on a ledge above the arena**, where a lone computer player could not reach them, so the arena never cleared (stages 3 and 5 hit the 15-minute cap). Found by the "never traps a squad" test. Dropped troopers now start under any ledge and land on the arena floor.
37. **Troopers worn down by a whirlwind or a slow burn stayed white** (the hit flash was set again every tick). Seen in screenshots; damage under 1 a tick from an astra no longer flashes.
38. **The bow could not be seen as an astra was loosed**: the astra's own flash was drawn over it. Seen in screenshots; the bow is drawn after the flash.
39. **A lone computer player that swapped its rain for another astra at a rest stop could not get past the wall of fire in stage 2** (it did not fetch rain from the altar and the run hit the time cap). Found by the "never traps a squad" test. Rest stops before a wall of fire now offer rain; the computer player's altar-fetching itself was not fixed.
40. **In the smallest CrazyGames frames (821x462, 800x450 on phones) the game fell back to the classic low-resolution art** (scale 1.155, HD started at 1.25). Found by capturing the game at the portal's listed frame sizes; HD now starts at 1.1.
41. **In a 907x510 frame the menu was cut off below "Play Astra Saga"** and a toast covered the menu's buttons. Found the same way; the compact menu now applies up to 640 px tall, and outside a match toasts sit top right.
42. **Pause was only on Escape**, which CrazyGames' guidelines say to avoid (in fullscreen it leaves fullscreen). P now pauses too, unless the player bound P to fire or jump.

## Not verified

- Pacing was measured with computer squads only (share of time with an enemy near, quiet stretches, astras loosed). Whether a stage now feels well paced to a person, and whether three charges plus regained charges make any boss too easy, was not playtested by people.

- The whirlwind, the fire, the bow and the battle arrays were checked by tests and in screenshots, not by people playing. Frame time with several whirlwinds and many burning troopers on screen was not measured. The daily trial was run by computer squads for seven days of dates, not for every date.

- Frame rates on real phones, Chromebooks and with a graphics card: the numbers above are from headless Chromium on a CPU. Eight real players over the internet were not tried; the simulation costs 0.1 ms a tick with eight players and a snapshot is about 5 KB (15 a second), measured with eight computer players.

Real phones, Firefox/Safari, sound by ear, a live deployment, the Docker image build, GitHub Actions, and how fun/difficult it is for people (the playtest uses computer players).
